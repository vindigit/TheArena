Game-authored work
TheArena adds rig-specific retargeting, cleanup, transitions, foot/contact solving, jump/release timing, and ball presentation. The reversible hand shapes are original game-authored deformations of Luke's existing geometry; the source motion capture does not provide these finger shapes. Luke's supplied model and its existing rights are unchanged. Existing model, texture, audio, and software notices remain applicable. None of the source parties necessarily endorses this game.
